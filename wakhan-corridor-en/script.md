# Wakhan Corridor: why does Afghanistan have a tail?

Total: ~62s

## s1  (0.0s – 4.1s)

**Voice:** Why does Afghanistan have this strange, long tail?

## s2  (4.1s – 11.4s)

**Voice:** It's called the Wakhan Corridor. A thin strip of land, about 350 kilometers long.

## s3  (11.4s – 19.0s)

**Voice:** To its north is Tajikistan. To the south, Pakistan. And at the very end, China.

## s4  (19.0s – 27.2s)

**Voice:** At one point, it's only 13 kilometers wide. Just a thin line of Afghanistan between Pakistan and Tajikistan.

## s5  (27.2s – 36.1s)

**Voice:** The reason? The Great Game. In the 1800s, British India and the Russian Empire were expanding toward each other.

## s6  (36.1s – 43.6s)

**Voice:** So in 1893 and 1895, treaties left this strip to Afghanistan, so the two empires would never touch.

## s7  (43.6s – 52.6s)

**Voice:** Its far end meets China. A border just 92 kilometers long, with the Wakhjir Pass nearly 4,900 meters high.

## s8  (52.6s – 61.9s)

**Voice:** Today, only about 18,000 people live here. A border drawn by two empires' fear. Follow for more map secrets!
